When a player receives an enchantment they do not want, they may salvage it and have a chance at receiving enchantment dust.

## Salvaging

By using `/salvage` to open the Salvage menu, players can click on unwanted books within their inventory to ready them for salvaging. 

Clicking the salvage button will recycle all readied enchantments. Recycled enchantments have a 50% chance of rewarding enchantment dust of its own tier.

## Dustforge

With enchantment dust, players can either craft shards, upgrade dust into higher tiers, or upgrade [Voidglass](/wiki/voidglass).

Crafting an enchantment shard will cost 5 of its tier's dust. Upgrading dust costs 10 of the current tier, as well as an Arcanium fragment for Legendary and Arcane dust.

Cracked Voidglass can be upgraded into Pure with 5 epic dust; and Pure Voidglass can be made into a Scroll of Void with 5 legendary dust and 5 paper.
