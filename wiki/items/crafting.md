## Overview

WitchCraftMC keeps vanilla crafting intact but adds a handful of **custom recipes** and [items](/wiki/categories/#items).

<div class="wiki-callout info">
  <span class="callout-icon"><i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i></span>
  <div>You can hover over any item in the recipes below for specific information. View all custom recipes in-game with <code>/recipes</code> or by checking the recipe book in your inventory.</div>
</div>

Since charcoal cannot be condensed into its own block, you can now craft it into a Block of Coal. Keep in mind that you cannot retransform it back into charcoal afterwards.
