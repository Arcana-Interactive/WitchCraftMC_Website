This page lists every custom Enchantment available to players. Enchantments you have discovered can also be viewed in-game with `/enchantjournal`.

---

## Obtaining

Enchanted books are found inside Enchantment Shards, which can be won from the [Enchanter Crate](/wiki/crates/#enchanter-crate). Right click a shard to open it for a random enchanted book of that rarity.

[Fishing enchantments](#fishing) are not found in shards. They can be bought with [Scales](/wiki/scales) from [Old Man Eyebrows](/wiki/npc/old-man-eyebrows).
