The Pyroclastic Core is an item that can be used in the crafting of [Pyroclastic](/wiki/pyroclastic/) Items.

## Obtaining

The Pyroclastic Core can be obtained rarely from the [Pyrite Crate](/wiki/crates#pyrite-crate).
