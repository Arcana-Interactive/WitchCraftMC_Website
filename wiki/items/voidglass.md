Voidglass items can be used to remove custom [enchantments](/wiki/enchantments) from enchanted items.

## Usage

To apply a Voidglass item, players can simply drag and drop it onto an item with a custom enchantment. This will open a GUI where they can select what enchantment to remove.

## Items

There are three types of Voidglass, Cracked Voidglass, Pure Voidglass and Scrolls of Void.

- Cracked Voidglass has a 50% chance of removing an enchantment.
- Pure Voidglass has a 100% chance of removing an enchantment.
- Scrolls of Void have a 100% chance of removing an enchantment, but also refund the enchanted book back to the player.

## Obtaining

Cracked Voidglass can be obtained from [Enchanter Crates](/wiki/crates#enchanter-crate) and [Pyrite Crate](/wiki/crates#pyrite-crate)

Pure Voidglass can be obtained by upgrading a Cracked Voidglass with 5 Epic Dust within the [Dustforge](/wiki/dustforge). 

Similarly, Scrolls of Void can be obtained by upgrading Pure Voidglass with 5 legendary dust and 5 paper in the Dustforge.
