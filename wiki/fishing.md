Fishing is one of the activities players can spend their time with. When a player fishes, they do not obtain vanilla fish; instead the fish are custom and can only be sold to [Old Man Eyebrows](/wiki/npc/old-man-eyebrows).

See `/fishjournal` or `/journal` to see the fish you've caught!

## Perks

When a player's fishing talent levels up, they will gain more perks.

### Levelable Skills
The following skills will increase in strength every 6 levels.
- **Lucky Catch** - Grants more fishing luck, which increases chance for extra drops.
- **Fisher** - Earn more Fishing Xp.
- **Treasure Hunter** - Higher chance to get rare loot from fishing.
- **Grappler** - Hook entities with more speed.
- **Epic Catch** - Extra chance to get epic loo tfrom fishing.
- **Sharp Hook** - Deal damage to a hooked entity when left clicking with a fishing rod.

Additionally, at level 5 Fishing, players gain access to `/fish shop`, which opens Old Man Eyebrows menu to sell fish remotely.

## Rarities
There are 6 rarities of fish players can fish up. Higher rarities are larger, and sell for more.

- **Common** - The default fish, unlocked immediately.
- **Junk** - Unlocked immediately.
- **Rare** - Unlocked at Level 10 Fishing.
- **Epic** - Unlocked at Level 20 Fishing.
- **Legendary** - Unlocked at Level 30 Fishing.
- **Mythical** - Unlocked at Level 40 Fishing.
- **Abyssal** - Requires 50 Fishing and Angler 5 Enchantment.

## Scales
When fishing, players can earn [Scales](/wiki/scales). Higher rarity fish give higher values of Scales.

Scales can be used at Old Man Eyebrows' shop to buy fishing gear.

## Competitions
Ocassionally fishing competitions will begin. All players online may attempt to win, the Top 5 players will receive some rewards.

Players can see when the next competitions is by enabling the Fishing Scoreboard with `/scoreboard`
