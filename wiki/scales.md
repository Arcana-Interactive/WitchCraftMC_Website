Scales are an additional currency aside from Money and (Kernels)[/wiki/kernels]. Like Kernels, Scales cannot be traded or given to other players.

They have a chance to be earned when [fishing](/wiki/fishing). Higher rarities of fish will earn players higher values of Scales.
