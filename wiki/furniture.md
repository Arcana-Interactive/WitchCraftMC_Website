## Overview

Furniture is decoration you can place around your base. Each piece has its own recipe, which you unlock once and can then craft as many times as you like.

## How to Get Furniture

1. **Visit [Steven Chairman](/wiki/npc/steven-chairman).** Use `/warp Furniture` to reach the shop in [Spawn](/wiki/spawn).
2. **Buy the design.** Click a piece in Steven's menu to pay its unlock price. The recipe stays unlocked for good.
3. **Gather the ingredients.** Each piece is listed below with what it needs.
4. **Craft it at a crafting table.** See the full layouts on the [Crafting Recipes](/wiki/crafting/#furniture-recipes) page, or use `/recipes` in-game.
